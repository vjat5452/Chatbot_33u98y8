What We Do

Experiential Journeys

Our Experience Centres are sophisticated, turnkey, phygital journeys that take you on a comprehensive survey of a project. Immerse yourself in the minutiae of the project, while being guided through a captivating narrative, empowering you to make an informed decision.

Read More

Immersive Environments

We create medium to large scale projection rooms and models that immerse audiences into the thick of a project’s ethos and story. Through new age technologies like VR and AR, we take users into the heart of the story. Read More

Architectural Visualisation

We render projects in 3D and create animated films that help our clients visualise and exhibit ideas by translating key information through emotionally resonant narratives. These films are made with a cinematic flourish, with storytelling as the anchor. Read More

Scale Models

Our life-like scale models offer audiences a precise representation of the projects they wish to interact with. The deft detailing and stunning craft ensure that our models exude a certain authenticity and life-like qualities of the actual structure. Read More

Interactive Solutions

We create touch tables, touch walls and other interactive solutions to help audiences navigate through the clutter of information in an engaging manner.  Without overwhelming, we try to present ideas in an engaging and impactful manner. Read More
